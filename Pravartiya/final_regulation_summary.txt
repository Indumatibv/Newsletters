The SEBI has issued amendments to the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations and introduced changes to governance requirements, disclosure obligations, compliance timelines, debt listing applicability thresholds, related party transaction norms, corporate governance reporting obligations, and procedural compliance requirements applicable to listed entities.

Sub domain: Regulations

Effective date(s) of circular:
  22.1.2026

Regulation Number: 15
  Gist of amendment: The threshold for applicability of governance provisions to high value debt listed entities has been raised from Rupees One Thousand Crore to Rupees Five Thousand Crore of outstanding listed non-convertible debt securities.
  Gist of Existing provisions of Law prior to amendment: The applicable threshold was Rupees One Thousand Crore of outstanding listed non-convertible debt securities.
  Gist of amendment: The provision related to the letter "a" has been substituted. A new provision has been inserted for high value debt listed entities which have an outstanding value of listed non-convertible debt securities of Rupees Five Thousand Crore and above. Such entities shall ensure compliance with certain provisions within six months from the date of such trigger, and the disclosures of such compliance may be made in the corporate governance compliance report on and from the third quarter following the date of the trigger.
  Gist of amendment: The 'comply or explain' requirement for the second proviso to sub-regulation (1A) of regulation 15 has been introduced. Entities shall endeavour to comply with the provisions and achieve full compliance by March 31, 2025.
  Gist of amendment: The regulation as it applies to a 'high value debt listed entity' will continue to be applicable until the value of the outstanding listed debt securities as on March 31 in a year, reduces and remains below the specified threshold for a period of three consecutive financial years. This is a substitution for the words "the regulation".

Regulation Number: 39
  Gist of amendment: The listed entity is now required to credit securities pursuant to investor service requests in relation to subdivision, split, consolidation, renewal, exchanges, and issuance of duplicate securities on account of loss or old decrepit or worn out certificates in dematerialised form within a period of thirty days from the date of receipt of such request along with relevant documents. This replaces the previous requirement for the listed entity to issue letter of confirmation or receipts or advices, as applicable, in cases of loss or old decrepit or worn out certificates or receipts or advices, as applicable, in dematerialised form within a period of thirty days from the date of such lodgement.

Regulation Number: 40
  Gist of amendment: The provision requiring that requests for effecting transfer of securities shall not be processed unless the securities are held in the dematerialised form with a depository, and transmission or transposition of securities held in physical or dematerialised form shall be effected only in dematerialised form, has been amended. The amendment allows for the registration of transfer of securities executed before April 01, 2019 and still held in physical form subject to such conditions as may be specified by the Board.
  Gist of Existing provisions of Law prior to amendment: "The listed entity shall also comply with the requirements as specified in this regulation for effecting transfer of securities: Provided that requests for effecting transfer of securities shall not be processed unless the securities are held in the dematerialised form with a depository: Provided further that transmission or transposition of securities held in physical or dematerialised form shall be effected only in dematerialised form."

Regulation Number: 61A
  Gist of amendment: The provision regarding transfer of unclaimed and unpaid amounts from escrow account has been revised. For all entities, the amount transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund will be in accordance with section 125 of the Companies Act, 2013 and rules made thereunder after seven years. For listed entities not falling within the definition of "company" under the Companies Act, 2013 and rules made thereunder, the amount in the escrow account that remains unclaimed and unpaid for a period of seven years from the maturity date of the non-convertible securities will be transferred to the Investor Protection and Education Fund created by the Board in terms of section 11 of the Act. The amount transferred to the Investor Protection and Education fund shall not bear any interest.
  Gist of Existing provisions of Law prior to amendment: Any amount transferred to the escrow account that remains unclaimed for seven years shall be transferred to the ‘Investor Education and Protection Fund’ constituted in terms of section 125 of the Companies Act, 2013 (for companies) or to the Investor Protection and Education Fund created by the Board in terms of section 11 of the Act (for listed entities not falling within the definition of “company” under the Companies Act, 2013 and the Rules made thereunder), with no provision specifying the time frame from the maturity date of non-convertible securities.

Regulation Number: 62C
  Gist of amendment: The word "One" has been replaced with no explicit prior provision mentioned in the given context. The provisions of this chapter now apply to a listed entity which only has non-convertible debt securities listed, with an outstanding value of Rupees 567[Five] Thousand Crore and above and does not have any listed specified securities.
  Gist of amendment: The word "One" has been replaced with a specified threshold value in Rupees (570 Crore) in the context of listed non-convertible debt securities. If the value of these securities equals or exceeds this threshold during a financial year, the listed entity must ensure compliance with certain provisions within six months from the trigger date and disclose such compliance in the third quarter following the trigger.
  Gist of amendment: Once a 'high value debt listed entity' is subject to this Chapter, the regulations shall continue to apply until the value of outstanding listed debt securities as on March 31 in a year, reduces and remains below the specified threshold for a period of three consecutive financial years. This provision overrides anything contained in sub-regulation (3) of regulation 3 that might suggest otherwise.
  Gist of amendment: The provisions of this sub-regulation will apply from April 01, 2025, except for entities that no longer meet the HVDLE criteria as per revised threshold set in Regulation 62C(1), to whom these provisions will not be applicable.

Regulation Number: 62D
  Gist of amendment: The term "explanatory statement" has been replaced with an unspecified new term in the context of a special resolution for appointing or continuing a non-executive director who has attained the age of seventy five years.
  Gist of amendment: The term "listed entity" has been substituted in the regulation. The provision regarding filling vacancies in the office of a director has been amended to include a condition that if the vacancy results in non-compliance with certain sub-regulations, compliance must be ensured at the earliest and not later than three months from the date of such vacancy. If the term of office of any director expires, resulting in a vacancy, the listed entity must fill the vacancy not later than the date of such office is vacated. However, this provision does not apply if the HVDLE fulfils the requirement under the relevant sub-regulations without filling the vacancy.
  Gist of amendment: The amendment introduces a provision stating that if the HVDLE becomes non-compliant with the requirement under sub-regulation (1) of this regulation due to the expiration of a director's term, the resulting vacancy shall be filled by the listed entity not later than the date of such office is vacated.

Regulation Number: 62K
  Gist of amendment: The HVDLE is now required to comply with regulation 23(1), (5), (6), and (7) in respect of related party transactions, as opposed to only complying with the provisions of regulation 23 above except for regulations 23(8) and 23(9).
  Gist of Existing provisions of Law prior to amendment: "The HVDLE shall formulate a policy on materiality of related party transactions and on dealing with related party transactions including clear threshold limits duly approved by the board of directors and such policy shall be reviewed by the board of directors at least once every three years and updated accordingly: Provided that a transaction with a related party shall be considered material, if the transaction(s) to be entered into individually or taken together with previous transactions during a financial year, exceeds Rupees one thousand crore or ten per cent. of the annual consolidated turnover of the listed entity as per the last audited financial statements of the listed entity, whichever is lower."
  Gist of amendment: The amendment introduces a requirement for all material related party transactions and subsequent material modifications to obtain a prior No-Objection Certificate from the Debenture Trustee. The Debenture Trustee is also required to obtain No-Objection from the debenture holders who are not related with the Issuer and hold atleast more than fifty per cent.
  Gist of amendment: The sub-regulations (3), (4) and (5) have been substituted. The provisions of sub-regulation (5) are no longer applicable in certain cases, including transactions between public sector companies, transactions between a holding company and its wholly owned subsidiary whose accounts are consolidated with such holding company, transactions between two wholly-owned subsidiaries of the listed holding company, transactions which are in the nature of payment of statutory dues, statutory fees or statutory charges entered into between an entity and the Central Government or any State Government, and transactions entered into between a public sector company and the Central Government or any State Government.
  Gist of amendment: The provisions of sub-regulation (5) under regulation 599 are not applicable in additional cases, specifically transactions entered into between two wholly-owned subsidiaries of a listed holding company, whose accounts are consolidated with such holding company and placed before the shareholders at the general meeting for approval.
  Gist of amendment: The term 'government company' as defined in sub-section (45) of section 2 of the Companies Act, 2013, has been substituted with a reference to a listed holding company in clause (a) of sub-regulation 604.

Regulation Number: 62L
  Gist of amendment: The term "material subsidiary" has been substituted for the word "income". A material subsidiary is now defined as a subsidiary whose turnover or net worth exceeds twenty per cent.
  Gist of amendment: The amendment introduces an exemption to sub-regulation 607, which states that this sub-regulation shall not be applicable if such sale, disposal or lease of assets is between two wholly-owned subsidiaries of the HVDLE.

Regulation Number: 62M
  Gist of amendment: The requirement for every HVDLE to submit a secretarial compliance report to stock exchanges within sixty days from end of each financial year, as per sub-regulation (2), has been omitted.
  Gist of Existing provisions of Law prior to amendment: "Every HVDLE shall submit a secretarial compliance report in such form as specified by the Board, to stock exchanges, within sixty days from end of each financial year."

Regulation Number: 62N
  Gist of amendment: The requirement for a new independent director to replace an existing one who resigns or is removed within three months has been omitted.
  Gist of Existing provisions of Law prior to amendment: "An independent director who resigns or is removed from the board of directors of the HVDLE shall be replaced by a new independent director by the HVDLE not later than three months from the date of such vacancy: Provided that where the HVDLE fulfils the requirement of independent directors in its board of directors without filling the vacancy created by such resignation or removal, the requirement of replacement by a new independent director shall not apply."

Regulation Number: 62P
  Gist of amendment: The amendment introduces a requirement that any vacancy in the office of Chief Executive Officer, Managing Director, Whole Time Director or Manager or Chief Financial Officer of a HVDLE (Highly leveraged debtor entity) in respect of which a resolution plan under section 31 of the Insolvency Code has been approved, shall be filled within a period of three months of such approval. In the interim, such HVDLE shall have not less than one full-time key managerial personnel managing its day-to-day affairs.

Regulation Number: 62Q
  Gist of amendment: The periodic compliance report on corporate governance submission timeline and format are now specified by the Board, as opposed to being within twenty-one days from the end of the period.
  Gist of amendment: The amendment introduces a new requirement for disclosure of details of cyber security incidents, breaches, loss of data or documents along with the report mentioned in clause (a) of the sub-regulation. Prior to this amendment, disclosure of material transactions with related parties was required.
  Gist of Existing provisions of Law prior to amendment: Details of all material transactions with related parties shall be disclosed along with the report mentioned in clause (a) of this sub-regulation. (This information is derived from CONTEXT A.)

Action point for listed entity if any:
  - A listed entity must ensure that it meets the new eligibility criteria as per the amended regulation, i.e., having non-convertible debt securities listed with an outstanding value of Rupees 567[Five] Thousand Crore and above and not having any listed specified securities.
  - A listed entity should review its compliance obligations related to the specified securities listed on the main board, SME Exchange, or Innovators Growth Platform to ensure they are in line with the updated regulations.
  - A listed holding company is now applicable to clause (a) of the relevant sub-regulation.
  - Entities are required to comply with the 'comply or explain' requirement for the second proviso to sub-regulation (1A) of regulation 15 and aim to achieve full compliance by March 31, 2025.
  - For listed entities that do not fall within the definition of "company" under the Companies Act, 2013 and rules made thereunder, the amendment requires them to consider the new seven-year time frame from the maturity date of non-convertible securities for transferring unclaimed amounts in escrow accounts to the Investor Protection and Education Fund created by the Board.
  - High value debt listed entities with an outstanding value of listed non-convertible debt securities of Rupees Five Thousand Crore and above should ensure compliance with certain provisions within six months from the date of such trigger, and make disclosures in the corporate governance compliance report on and from the third quarter following the date of the trigger.
  - If a listed entity has wholly-owned subsidiaries, it may no longer be subject to the requirements of the relevant sub-regulation when engaging in sales, disposals or leases of assets between these subsidiaries.
  - If a resolution plan under section 31 of the Insolvency Code has been approved for a HVDLE, the listed entity should ensure that the vacancy in the office of Chief Executive Officer, Managing Director, Whole Time Director or Manager or Chief Financial Officer is filled within three months. During this period, the HVDLE must have at least one full-time key managerial personnel managing its day-to-day affairs.
  - If a special resolution is passed to appoint or continue a non-executive director who has attained the age of seventy five years, the justification for such appointment should be indicated in the annexed explanatory statement attached to the notice for the motion.
  - If a vacancy in the office of a director arises due to the expiration of their term, the listed entity must fill the vacancy not later than the date of such office is vacated.
  - If the value of outstanding listed non-convertible debt securities becomes equal to or greater than [specified threshold] during a financial year, the listed entity must comply with the specified provisions within six months from the trigger date and disclose such compliance in the third quarter following the trigger.
  - Implement the process of obtaining No-Objection Certificate from the Debenture Trustee and approval from the required percentage of debenture holders not related to the Issuer, for all material related party transactions and subsequent material modifications.
  - Listed entities are now required to disclose details of cyber security incidents, breaches, loss of data or documents along with the report mentioned in clause (a) of the sub-regulation as specified.
  - Maintain and monitor the value of outstanding listed debt securities to ensure it remains below the specified threshold for a period of three consecutive financial years, as failure to do so may result in continued application of regulations 15 to 27.
  - No longer required to submit a secretarial compliance report to stock exchanges as per the specified timeline.
  - Review and update internal policies and procedures related to transactions between wholly-owned subsidiaries, ensuring compliance with the newly introduced exemption from sub-regulation (5) under the relevant regulation.
  - Review and update the definition of "material subsidiary" in their internal policies and procedures according to the new definition.
  - Review the impact of this amendment on transactions that fall under the aforementioned categories and adjust compliance accordingly.
  - Review their status as HVDLEs according to the revised threshold in Regulation 62C(1) to determine whether they are subject to the provisions of this sub-regulation from April 01, 2025.
  - The listed entity is now allowed to register transfer of securities executed before April 01, 2019 and still held in physical form subject to conditions specified by the Board.
  - The listed entity is now required to credit securities instead of issuing letter of confirmation or receipts or advices in certain cases as per the new regulation.
  - The listed entity must ensure compliance with the specified sub-regulations when a vacancy in the office of a director occurs, and fill the vacancy not later than the date of such office is vacated if the term of office expires. If the HVDLE fulfils the requirement under the relevant sub-regulations without filling the vacancy, this provision does not apply.
  - The listed entity should comply with the new timeline and format for submitting periodic compliance reports on corporate governance as specified by the Board.
  - The listed entity should review and update its policy on materiality of related party transactions and dealing with related party transactions in accordance with the new requirements under regulation 23(1), (5), (6), and (7).
