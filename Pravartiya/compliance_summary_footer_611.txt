Gist of amendment:
A new requirement has been introduced for the HVDLE to fill any vacancy in the office of the Chief Financial Officer at the earliest and in any case not later than three months from the date of such vacancy, unless otherwise specified. If approval of regulatory, government or statutory authorities is required, the vacancy shall be filled up by the HVDLE at the earliest and in any case not later than six months from the date of vacancy. The HVDLE is also prohibited from appointing a person in interim capacity unless such appointment is made in accordance with the laws applicable in case of a new appointment to such office and the obligations under such laws are made applicable to such person.

Existing provisions of Law prior to amendment:
Not explicitly mentioned

Action point for listed entity if any:
The listed entity should ensure that any vacancy in the office of the Chief Financial Officer is filled in accordance with the new requirements introduced by the amendment, and that any interim appointments are made in compliance with the applicable laws. The entity should also update its internal governance records, trackers, timelines, exemptions, or compliance references to reflect the new provisions.
